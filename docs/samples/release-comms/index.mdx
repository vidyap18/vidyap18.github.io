# Release communication sample

<span className="vp-label">Original sample, re-created for a fictional product.</span>

**Audience:** five pieces for five readers of one feature: customers scanning release notes, planning leads who need the full picture in a minute, every customer in a pre-release inbox, customer success managers fielding questions, and the presenter and editor making the walkthrough video.

**What I decided and why:** I led every piece with what does not change, because the rollout's real risk was anxiety about existing work rather than confusion about the new feature, and I held one vocabulary (Build, Live, promote, approved settings) across all five so a customer could move between them without translating.

**What I'd do next:** test the one-minute briefing with two planning leads before rollout, fold their first questions into the FAQ, and add a "day after" check-in note for customer success managers.
