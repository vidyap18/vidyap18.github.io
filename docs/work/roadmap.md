# A roadmap leadership can read

<span className="vp-eyebrow">Roadmap communication · in use since September 2026</span>

**What.** A dashboard fed from Jira across three product areas: the core platform, AI agents, and forecasting and optimisation algorithms. Each epic shows a plain-English update, a status and a quarter, with filters by area and status. A customer-facing version shows only what can be shared.

**Why it mattered.** Leadership and customer success needed the roadmap without reading engineering tickets. The dashboard now feeds the CEO's weekly update and was used for a roadmap presentation at a customer site.

## My part

I took over the dashboard in September 2026 and rebuilt it with the product managers who use it. I wrote a Jira field guide so product managers keep their epics up to date, and set a two-week cadence around it: roadmap review, top customer requests, and release confirmation, each with an owner and a definition of done. About 190 epics now carry a plain-English update.

## What this shows

Making a source of truth readable for the people who decide from it.
