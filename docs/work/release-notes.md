# Release notes and release videos, every two weeks since February 2025

<span className="vp-eyebrow">Release notes · every release since February 2025</span>

![Redesigned release notes page](/img/work/release-notes-redesign.png)

**What.** I own release communication. Between February 2025 and October 2026 that was more than 40 releases, roughly one every two weeks. For each one I wrote the company-wide announcement and wrote or edited the release notes on the docs site.

## The rollout package

The largest single piece was the introduction of separate development and production environments in September 2026. I wrote:

- a customer briefing that explains the change in one minute, what changes on rollout day, a five-step migration, and what stays editable in production;
- the customer email and the internal announcement;
- a briefing for customer success, through seven drafts;
- the brief for a 3:38 walkthrough video presented by the product manager, and I produced the video.

A re-creation of this package for a fictional product is in the [release communication sample](/samples/release-comms).

## Formats I introduced

- **A two-host podcast** for major releases, from March 2025: scripted with AI and voiced synthetically. [First episode](https://lyric.wistia.com/medias/lwy45h61u4).
- **Video release notes**, from April 2026, after the CEO asked for them: the first walkthrough presented by a product manager, plus an interactive demo. I wrote the brief: a four-beat structure, a questionnaire for the presenter, tone rules and edit notes.
- **A redesigned release-notes page**, September 2026: a highlights panel, navigation and filters by release, and a top-five section for each release.

16 releases now have walkthrough videos.

> "The release notes we publish showcase a great personality." Founder and CEO, in the company-wide channel

## What this shows

Release writing on a fixed cadence, and the production work for change communication: briefings, email, video.
