<h3>Syncing and committing scenarios</h3>

A planner works in a copy of a plan and, sooner or later, has to pull in a colleague's changes or push their own back. Both operations can overwrite work. The guide opens with a side-by-side comparison so readers choose the right operation first. It names the most common mistake, the record-level trap, and ends with a checklist to run before every commit.
