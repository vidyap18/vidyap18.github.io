Optimize Network

The Optimize Network Note optimizes distribution networks, facility locations, capacity planning, and sourcing decisions — configure inputs, the optimizer minimizes cost subject to constraints, and it returns plans and KPIs.
Inputs

The Product UoM table lets you plan in the units your business actually uses, and this version adds cross-border tax support — see the dedicated Tax Duty page.

ColumnDescription
product_idUnique product identifier
base_unitThe unit this product's demand/inventory is entered in
tax_regionAssigns this product/location to a tax region for duty calculation New in 4.1
Parameters · Run Modes
  • Cost Minimization
  • Profit Maximization
  • Incremental Run
  • Multi-Unit Planning
  • Tariff-Aware Sourcing New in 4.1
Outputs
  • Location Product Flow Report
  • Lane Aggregated Report incl. Asset Utilization
  • Tax Duty Report New in 4.1

Tax Duty New in 4.1

Define import tariffs and duties on material that crosses a tax-region boundary. Tariffs are included in total cost, so the optimizer re-routes and re-sources to avoid high duties.
ColumnDescription
tax_regionThe tax region this duty rate applies to
basisProductOrigin or ShipmentLane
rate_typeAd-valorem (% of value) or a specific per-unit charge
